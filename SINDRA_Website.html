About SINDRA

A connection layer between two semiconductor ecosystems

SINDRA stands for Semiconductor India Israel. Its purpose is to connect Israeli capabilities to Indian opportunities.

India's build-out needs suppliers, talent, infrastructure and process know-how. Israel brings tech depth, fab experience and manufacturing capability, with existing ties to India. SINDRA connects the two through the SINDRA Interest Office.

IESA – India Electronics & Semiconductor Association

Who is IESA
India's premier body for electronics design & manufacturing (ESDM) and semiconductors – the government's main industry knowledge partner.
Mission
Make India the preferred global destination for semiconductor and electronics design and manufacturing, across the full value chain.
What they do
Advocacy with governments, industry data, skilling (SemiconIndia FutureSkills), startup & MSME support and global alliances such as SEMI.
Why they are relevant
The single front door to India's chip ecosystem – fabs, OSAT/ATMP players, policymakers and talent – under the ISM build-out.
